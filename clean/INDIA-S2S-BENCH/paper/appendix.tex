\section{Supplementary protocol and diagnostics}

\subsection{Complete coverage}

The common initialization counts are 35 in each of 2020--2023, 30 in 2024,
and 35 in 2025. Exact ISO dates are stored in
\texttt{coverage.csv}; per-model availability and source metadata hashes are
stored in \texttt{preflight/coverage\_preflight.csv} and
\texttt{preflight/preflight.json}. The metadata-only preflight records
\texttt{forecast\_values\_read=false}.

\subsection{Claim gates}

The headline requires positive lower 95\% ACC endpoints against both equal
weighting and the validation-selected individual system, a positive
forecast-only versus location-only endpoint, at most 5\% relative RMSE
regression, at most 0.5 mm day$^{-1}$ absolute-bias regression, and positive
regional ACC deltas in at least three of four IMD regions. All pass except the
selected-model ACC endpoint, -0.002. The machine-readable decision is in
\texttt{gate\_report.json}.

\subsection{Hyperparameters and compute}

XGBoost uses squared-error loss, histogram trees, CPU execution, maximum depth
4, minimum child weight 5, learning rate 0.03, row subsampling 0.8, column
subsampling 0.9, $L_2$ regularization 1, and seed 42. The maximum tree count is
600 with patience 50. The exact selected count, feature names, and row counts
for every lead/variant are in \texttt{fit\_metadata.json}. Runtime and energy
were not instrumented reliably enough for a numerical emissions claim.

\subsection{Neural correction exclusion}

The available FuXi neural-correction control defines Week 1 as initialization
through initialization+6 days, while every benchmark forecast defines daily
lead 1 as the accumulation interval ending initialization+1 day. Applying its
saved model or scores here would shift labels by one day. It is excluded from
the common-date result and may only be revisited after training under the
frozen +1--+42 endpoint contract.

\subsection{Reproduction commands}

\begin{verbatim}
PYTHONPATH=src python run.py preflight
PYTHONPATH=src python run.py run
PYTHONPATH=src python run.py audit
PYTHONPATH=src python make_paper.py
\end{verbatim}

The runner refuses an existing output directory. A repeated test must use a
new path and is a distinct confirmatory run, never a replacement chosen by
score. SHA256 values for every saved artifact appear in
\texttt{manifest.json}.
