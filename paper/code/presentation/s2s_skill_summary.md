# 8. Key Findings & Flags

## 8.1 What Looks Good ✅
- **TP (Precipitation)**: All models show positive PCC through Week 4–5 over All India. CRPSS is positive for all models through Week 3–4. Results are physically consistent.
- **Z500 PCC (Week 1–2)**: SPIRE (0.94/0.87), ECMWF (0.94/0.84), and NCEP (0.92/0.63) all show high skill, consistent with known S2S predictability windows.
- **SPIRE overall**: Consistently top performer across all variables and regions in both PCC and CRPSS.
- **MME (Multi-Model Ensemble)**: Beats individual models on most metrics, which is expected.

## 8.2 Red Flags 🔴
- **T2M MSSS for FuXi/ECMWF/NCEP**: Deeply negative (−10 to −40) across all regions from Week 1. This is not physical — it strongly suggests a **T2M climatology or unit mismatch**. Models should at minimum beat climatology in Week 1.
- **FuXi Z500 skill collapse**: PCC drops from 0.82 (Wk1) to near 0 by Week 3 and goes negative. Much faster than ECMWF or SPIRE. Possible grid or unit issue in Z500 loading.
- **T2M CRPSS (FuXi/ECMWF/NCEP)**: Values of −2 to −2.5 indicate ensemble spread is severely underestimated relative to actual errors (SSR << 1), confirming a calibration problem for T2M.
- **NCEP Z500 MSSS South Peninsula**: Extremely negative values (−17 to −30) from Week 2 onward suggest a data loading or masking issue specific to NCEP.

## 8.3 Action Items 🔧
1. **Investigate T2M climatology**: The ERA5 30-yr day-of-year climatology used for scoring — check that T2M truth and forecasts are on the same definition (daily mean vs. instantaneous).
2. **Check FuXi Z500 units**: Verify that `z500 / g` conversion is applied consistently.
3. **Check NCEP South Peninsula**: Possible NaN masking or land-sea mask mismatch for NCEP.
4. **T2M probabilistic spread**: All models (except SPIRE) have SSR << 1 for T2M — investigate if ensemble perturbations in T2M space are too small.
